Privacy

King of the Slams doesn't collect your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and there is no server of ours that receives your data.

What stays on your phone

Your trade, language level, character, work week, reminder settings, saved items, reactions, and approximate location (city, state, and coordinates rounded to about a kilometer) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or using Account → Reset my data, removes them.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Weather. To show your forecast, the app sends your rounded coordinates to the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). If you entered a ZIP code, it sends that ZIP to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (data.epa.gov) for the UV index. Neither request carries a name, account, or device identifier.
  • News. The app reads publishers' public news feeds directly and loads each story's thumbnail and the site's icon from that publisher, like opening their website. Publishers see a normal web request from your phone, such as its IP address.
  • Location lookup. If you use your location or a ZIP code, Apple's geocoding service turns it into a city and state.
  • Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your phone and never leave it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
