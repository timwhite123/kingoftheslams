Support

Something not working, or a joke that missed? Open an issue and we'll take a look.

Change your trade, language, or character

Tap the profile button on Today, then Account.

Change or stop the daily reminder

Account → Reminders. Turn it off, pick days, or change how long before your shift it comes.

No weather or local news

Add a location in Account → Location (your location or a ZIP code). Weather covers the United States.

A joke or headline isn't for you

Tap ••• on it and choose Not for me, Too harsh, or Report. It won't show again on your phone.

Erase everything

Account → Reset my data.
